User Guide

How to log in, add news stories and reports, upload photos, and keep the Obudu Conservation Centre website up to date — without needing a developer.

Quick start

The five tasks you'll do most often

  1. Log in — go to obuduconservationc.org/admin, enter email and password.
  2. Post a news story — Admin → News → New post → fill the form → Create post.
  3. Add a report with a PDF — Admin → Reports → New report → attach the PDF → Create report.
  4. Add photos to the gallery — Admin → Gallery → Upload → drag photos → Upload all.
  5. Edit or delete — Admin → the list of the item type → Edit or Delete next to the row.

01

What the CMS is for

The CMS (short for Content Management System) is the hidden back-of-house part of the OCC website that lets staff add and change content without touching code. You open it in a browser, sign in, and work in simple forms. When you save, the changes appear on the public site straight away.

What you can manage yourself

  • News posts — stories, updates and announcements that appear on the News page.
  • Reports — longer pieces of writing, usually with a downloadable PDF attached, that appear on the Reports page.
  • Gallery photos — individual photographs that appear on the Gallery page.

What you can't change yourself (contact the developer)

  • The page copy on Home, About, Donate, Founder's Story and Team pages
  • The team member list (names, roles, photos, biographies)
  • Hero images and background photos on pages (the big photos at the top of each page)
  • Navigation menu items, the footer, and the OCC logo
  • Site settings like the donation account details

These are set in the website's code and need the developer to change them. If something on one of those pages is wrong or needs updating, use the contact information in Section 13.

02

Logging in

  1. Open your web browser and go to https://obuduconservationc.org/admin
  2. Enter the email address you were given.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Click Sign in. You will be taken to the Dashboard.
The login screen (email + password + Sign in button)

If you forget your password

There is no self-service "forgot password" link. Contact the developer (see Section 13) and ask for a password reset. The developer will set a new one and tell you what it is on a secure channel; change it on your side afterwards.

If the login won't accept your credentials

Double-check that caps lock is off, that the email is spelled correctly, and that you are copying the password without extra spaces. The system does not lock your account after failed attempts, so you can keep trying. If you still cannot get in, contact the developer.

Signing out

Use the Sign out link in the top-right of any admin screen. On a shared or public computer, always sign out when you are done.

03

Adding a News post

News posts appear on the public News page at obuduconservationc.org/news.

  1. From the Dashboard, click the News card.
  2. Click New post (top right of the News list).
  3. Fill in the form (every field explained below).
  4. Click Create post.
The New Post form filled in with example content

Fields on the News form

Title Required
The headline of the story. Short and specific is better than long and clever. e.g. "Primate monitoring programme — Q3 2026 update"
Slug
A short, URL-safe version of the title that becomes part of the web address. It is filled in for you automatically from the title; you normally don't need to touch it. Only edit it if you want a shorter or different URL. e.g. "primate-monitoring-q3-2026-update"
Date published
Pick a date from the calendar. This is the date shown next to the post on the News page. Defaults to today if you leave it blank.
Thumbnail
A cover image for the post, shown on the News page card and at the top of the article when a visitor opens it. Click Choose file and pick a photo from your computer. See Section 9 for file-size and format rules.
Alt text
A one-sentence description of the thumbnail photo. Used by screen readers for people who cannot see the image, and by search engines. Describe what is in the picture. e.g. "Volunteer tagging a Preuss's Monkey at the Obudu research camp."
Excerpt
A short summary — two or three sentences — shown on the News page card. Think of it as the invitation to read the full post. Keep it under 300 characters.
Body
The full content of the story. Write in plain prose. Separate paragraphs with a blank line (press Enter twice). There is no formatting toolbar — if you need bold, italics or links, flag it with the developer for a future upgrade.

After clicking Create post you'll return to the News list and the new post appears at the top. Open the public News page in another browser tab to see it live.

04

Adding a Report

Reports appear on the public Reports page at obuduconservationc.org/reports. A report usually has a title, a short write-up, a cover image, and a downloadable file (typically a PDF).

  1. From the Dashboard, click the Reports card.
  2. Click New report.
  3. Fill in the form (fields below).
  4. Click Create report.
The Report form with cover image and PDF attached

Fields on the Report form

Title Required
The title of the report. e.g. "Annual Biodiversity Monitoring Report 2026"
Date
The publication date of the report.
Cover image
A photograph shown next to the write-up on the Reports page. Same file rules as news thumbnails (Section 9).
Alt text
A one-sentence description of the cover image.
Description
The body text shown on the Reports page. Think of this as the summary a visitor reads before deciding to download the full report. Keep it to a paragraph or two.
Downloadable file Optional
Attach the full report as a PDF (Word documents also accepted). Click Choose file and pick it from your computer. Maximum file size is 3 MB — see Section 9 for how to compress larger files.

Shrinking a large PDF

If your PDF is over 3 MB, use a free online compressor such as Smallpdf or iLovePDF before uploading. Choose a "medium" or "web" quality level; a 10 MB report will usually come down to 1–2 MB without visible loss.

05

Adding Gallery photos

The Gallery page at obuduconservationc.org/gallery displays photographs OCC wants to share with the public. The admin lets you upload many photos at once.

  1. From the Dashboard, click the Gallery card.
  2. Click Upload.
  3. Drag photos from your computer onto the drop area, or click Choose files and select them. You can pick as many as you like in one go.
  4. For each photo in the queue, write a short alt text (one sentence describing what is in the picture).
  5. Click Upload all. The system uploads them one by one and marks each as Done or Failed.
  6. When finished, click Back to gallery.
The Gallery upload queue with photos ready to send
Tip Nothing is uploaded until you click Upload all. Up to that point you can remove any file from the queue with the Remove button, add more photos, or edit the alt text freely.

If a photo fails to upload

The most common reason is file size (over 3 MB). The failed row stays in the queue with a red error message explaining why. Remove it, shrink the file (see Section 9), and add it again.

06

Editing an existing item

  1. From the Dashboard, click the card for the type of item (News, Reports, or Gallery).
  2. Find the item in the list and click Edit.
  3. Change the fields you want to update.
  4. Click Save changes.
Images If you don't pick a new image or file, the existing one is kept. You only need to re-upload if you actually want to replace it.

Changes appear on the public site immediately. If a visitor already has the page open in their browser, they may need to refresh (Section 12 covers this).

07

Deleting an item

  1. From the Dashboard, click the card for the type of item (News, Reports, or Gallery).
  2. Find the item in the list and click Delete.
  3. Confirm when the browser asks.
Warning Deletion cannot be undone from the admin. The item disappears from the public site immediately. Recent items may still be recoverable from the monthly backup for a short window — if you delete something by mistake, contact the developer straight away (see Section 13). The faster you report it, the better the chance of recovery.

If you want to temporarily hide an item instead of deleting it, don't delete — ask the developer about adding a draft/hidden state (a planned upgrade, not currently in the admin).

08

When changes go live

The CMS does not have a separate "draft" and "published" state. Whatever you save is instantly live on the public website. There is no approval step and no scheduled publishing.

Visitors see your changes on their next page load. There is no cache that holds the old version for several minutes.

If a visitor has the page already open in their browser from before you saved, they'll still see the old version until they refresh. This is browser behaviour, not the CMS. See Section 12 for the keyboard shortcut.

Rule of thumb Treat "Save changes" and "Create post" the same way you'd treat sending a public tweet: once you click, it's visible to anyone in the world.
09

File and image rules

Maximum file size

3 MB per upload, hard limit. Files bigger than this are rejected with an error message naming the file and its size.

Images

FormatBehaviour
JPEG, PNG, WebPAutomatically shrunk in your browser to 2000 pixels on the longest side and re-saved as a smaller JPEG before being uploaded. You can upload originals straight from a camera; the system handles the shrinking.
HEIC (iPhone photos)Not auto-shrunk. The raw file must already be under 3 MB. Easiest fix: on your iPhone, export or send the photo as "JPEG" or "Most Compatible" before uploading.
GIF, SVGNot auto-shrunk. Must already be under 3 MB.

Report attachments

FormatBehaviour
PDF, DOC, DOCXNot auto-shrunk. Must be under 3 MB. For bigger PDFs, use Smallpdf or iLovePDF first.

Recommended image dimensions

  • Minimum: 1200 pixels wide, so the photo looks crisp on larger screens.
  • Maximum useful size: anything bigger than 2000 pixels is automatically shrunk.
  • Aspect ratio: landscape (wider than tall) works best for thumbnails and cover images, roughly 3:2 or 4:3.

Filenames and alt text

  • Give files human-readable names: primate-rescue-july-2026.jpg, not IMG_8734.jpg.
  • Alt text should describe what's actually in the photo. "A ranger and a child planting a seedling" is useful. "Photo 1" is not.
10

Good practice

Write for the reader

  • Specific titles beat clever ones. "Primate monitoring programme — Q3 2026 update" tells someone exactly what they'll read; "An update" tells them nothing.
  • Short excerpts that answer "why should I click?" work better than a repeated title.
  • Dates in full. Pick the real publication date so visitors can see what's recent.

Check your work

  • Open the public page (/news, /reports or /gallery) in a new tab after saving.
  • Refresh. Confirm the title, image, and excerpt read correctly.
  • Click into the full post or report; make sure the body text reads well and the PDF (if any) downloads.

Protect sensitive information

Do not publish without approval
  • Unpublished research data or draft findings
  • Precise GPS locations of endangered species or active research sites
  • Confidential partner or funder information
  • Personal data (names, phone numbers, addresses) of individuals who haven't consented to be named
  • Images of children where parental consent hasn't been obtained
If in doubt, don't publish — ask OCC's management first.
11

Security

Protect your login

  • Never share your email and password with anyone — not even a colleague, "just this once". If a colleague needs access, ask the developer to create a separate login for them.
  • Use a strong password. A password manager (such as 1Password or Bitwarden, both free for personal use) generates and remembers them for you.
  • Change it on a schedule. Every 6–12 months is a sensible cadence. Ask the developer to generate a new one.

Two-step sign-in

The admin login does not currently support two-step verification (also called 2FA or MFA). This is a planned upgrade. In the meantime, a strong unique password matters more than usual — do not reuse the OCC admin password anywhere else.

On shared or public computers

  • Always click Sign out before closing the browser or leaving the computer.
  • Do not tick "remember me" boxes if the browser offers them.
  • Avoid using the admin on public Wi-Fi without a secure connection.

If you suspect someone else has accessed the account

Act immediately
  1. Tell the developer and OCC management straight away.
  2. The developer will reset the password and sign out all active sessions.
  3. Review recent News, Reports, and Gallery items for anything you don't recognise.
The faster the password is reset, the less damage anyone can do.
12

Troubleshooting

Most problems have simple fixes. Try the suggested step first; if it doesn't work, contact the developer with the exact wording of any error message you saw.

If this happens…Try this
Login won't accept your email and password Check caps lock, copy the password without extra spaces, and make sure the email matches what you were given exactly. If still blocked, contact the developer for a reset.
Upload rejected — "file too large" The file is over 3 MB. For photos, export at smaller dimensions or lower quality. For PDFs, shrink with Smallpdf or iLovePDF first. See Section 9.
Your changes aren't showing on the public page Hard-refresh the public page in your browser. On Mac: Cmd + Shift + R. On Windows / Linux: Ctrl + Shift + R. If still absent after a minute, log into the admin and confirm the change was really saved.
You deleted the wrong thing Contact the developer immediately. Recent items may be recoverable from the monthly backup, but speed matters.
An error message appears when you save Write down the exact wording and send it to the developer. Do not re-submit the form repeatedly — if the first save actually succeeded despite the error, re-submitting creates duplicates.
The admin page itself won't load Try a different browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge). If still broken, check that obuduconservationc.org loads at all. If the main site also fails, contact the developer — it's a hosting issue, not a CMS one.
